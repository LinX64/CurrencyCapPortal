Prices look stale or wrong

Gheymat shows a freshness indicator at the top of the Prices screen. The upstream market data updates on its own schedule and is not always live by the second; the app shows exactly when it last updated. If it has been stuck for a long time, pull down on the Prices screen to refresh, or check your internet connection.

Is this financial advice?

No. Gheymat shows market prices and a simple trend line computed from recent history. Nothing in the app is financial advice, and the trend estimate is not a prediction you should rely on for financial decisions.

Why can't I see 40 years of history for gold or coins?

Daily history going back to 2012 is available for currencies. Gold, coins and crypto currently only have the history the app itself has collected since you started using it; older daily history for those is on our roadmap.
